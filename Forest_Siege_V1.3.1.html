Controls & rules

1–9 / 0 / - / Q / W / E / R / Y: select defense.
F: aim fireball. G: Astral Pulse. T: deploy troops.
N: skip preparation. A: autoplay.
Space: start or pause. Esc: cancel.
Reward screen: continue.
Intro splash: Space/Enter to begin.

Placement snaps to a 50-unit grid. Click a valid cell to place. Drag any defense during prep to reposition it. Towers can merge into stronger tiers by placing or dragging a duplicate onto the same tower type during prep.

Towers gain mastery experience across runs. Enemy types are logged to the codex when encountered.
